# tally — Frequently Asked Questions

## Is tally really free?

Yes. Running tally on your own computer is free and always will be. The only thing
that costs money is the optional hosted backup add-on.

## How many expenses can I track on the free plan?

The free plan includes hosted backup for up to **100 expenses per month**. You can
record more than that locally — nothing stops you — but only the first 100 in a
month are covered by the free backup. The Plus and Family plans remove the limit.

## Where is my data kept?

In a single JSON file on your own machine. Unless you turn on the hosted backup
add-on, your data never leaves your computer.

## Can I enter expenses in other currencies?

Yes. Pass `--currency` when you add an expense and tally converts it to US dollars
for you.
