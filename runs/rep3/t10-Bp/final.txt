**What was done:**

**`tally/cli.py`** — three targeted changes:
1. **Top-level exception handler** in `main()`: wraps `args.func(args)` in `try/except (ValueError, OSError)` and `json.JSONDecodeError`, printing `error: <message>` to stderr and returning exit code 1.
