## Final report

**What the bug was:** The `--week` option didn't exist at all. The request described the classic Sunday-start mistake that happens when week numbers are computed with `strftime('%W')` or `%U`. The correct fix is to use Python's `date.isocalendar()`, which implements ISO 8601 — weeks run Monday (weekday=1) through Sunday (weekday=7).
