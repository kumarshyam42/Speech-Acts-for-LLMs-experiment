**Final report**

**What changed:** `format_report` in `tally/report.py` was enriched with three additional columns per category row:

1. **Transaction count** — `2 items` / `1 item` (singular/plural), shown on every category row and on the TOTAL row.
2. **Share of period total** — `XX.X%`, so you can see at a glance which categories dominate the spend.
3. **Budget utilisation** — `budget: 600.00 (15.4% used)`, shown only when a month filter is active (since an unscoped total has no natural budget period). Categories with no defined budget silently omit this column.

A private helper `_category_stats` was added to accumulate both totals and counts in one pass; the public `category_totals` function is untouched (callers like `monthly_summary` continue to work without change).
