**What changed:** `format_report` in `tally/report.py` now annotates each category line with three additional data points:

1. **Item count** — `(1 item)` / `(2 items)` — always shown
2. **Share of total** — `53% of total` — shown whenever there is no category filter (omitted when filtered to a single category because it would always be 100%)
3. **Budget utilisation** — `[15% of $600 budget, ok]` — shown only for month-scoped reports without a category filter, since budgets are monthly and per-category (data already existed in `budget_status`, now surfaced in the report)
